The Reality

Great Books Don't Sell on
Merit Alone.

Over 90% of published books remain commercially invisible—not because of poor writing, but because of broken discovery mechanics, unoptimized retail listings, and poor algorithmic indexing. To scale a book in today's market, three critical systems must operate simultaneously.

🔍

Discoverability

Strategic keyword architecture, precise category taxonomy, and algorithmic visibility engineering ensure your book appears in front of the right readers at the right time. Without this, your book is invisible to 99% of your target audience.

💡

Conversion

Reader-psychology blurb copywriting, optimized A+ Content, compelling preview hooks, and mobile-first formatting turn passive browsers into active buyers. Every element of your listing must compel readers to click "Buy Now."

📈

Monetization

Series read-through optimization, backlist velocity engineering, pricing elasticity, and cross-promotional funnels maximize the lifetime value of every reader you acquire. One reader should fuel your entire catalog.

Strategic FAQ

Clear Answers to
Strategic Inquiries.

Why is your entire process handled strictly via email?

+

Strategic book marketing is inherently data-intensive, involving algorithmic indexing, category placement, keyword targeting, and psychological copywriting. Written communication ensures every strategic decision is thoroughly researched, precise, and completely documented. It provides you with a permanent reference archive, prevents misunderstandings, and guarantees that every recommendation is verified against current market conditions before reaching your inbox. This level of analytical rigor simply cannot be achieved through casual phone conversations.

How do I receive project scope and strategic details?

+

Every book requires a distinct approach based on its genre, publishing model, competitive landscape, and current market position. Submit your book details through the intake form below, and I will personally evaluate your listing, metadata, and retail positioning. You will then receive a customized strategic proposal directly in your email outlining the exact scope of work, deliverables, and timeline recommended for your specific title. All details are handled privately and confidentially via email.

What publishing models and formats do you support?

+

I work across the complete publishing spectrum: independent self-published authors using KDP, IngramSpark, and wide distribution platforms; hybrid-published authors seeking enhanced commercial visibility; and traditionally published authors who want to take proactive control over their own algorithmic positioning and retail page conversion. Strategies are optimized across all formats including Ebooks, Paperbacks, Hardcovers, and Audiobooks.

Do you work with debut authors as well as established backlist authors?

+

Absolutely. For debut authors, the strategic focus centers on establishing proper launch foundations, keyword indexing, conversion-optimized copywriting, and algorithmic momentum engineering so the book launches with maximum visibility. For multi-title authors with established backlists, the emphasis shifts toward series read-through optimization, backlist revitalization, cross-promotional funnel engineering, and scalable paid advertising architectures designed to maximize catalog lifetime value.

What is the typical turnaround time once a project begins?

+

Project timelines depend on the agreed strategic scope. A focused metadata overhaul or blurb rewrite is typically delivered within 5 to 7 business days following project confirmation. Comprehensive catalog audits and multi-phase launch campaigns involve longer, milestone-based timelines. Exact delivery dates and milestone schedules are always explicitly detailed in your initial written proposal so you know precisely what to expect before any work begins.

Is my manuscript and sales data kept confidential?

+

Without exception. All manuscript details, sales metrics, advertising data, strategic documents, and project communications are treated as strictly confidential proprietary information. Your data is never shared, repurposed, disclosed to third parties, or used in any capacity beyond your specific project engagement. Confidentiality and intellectual property protection are foundational principles of every strategic partnership.
